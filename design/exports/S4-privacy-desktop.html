⚡ FileBrik

Privacy Policy & Architecture

Effective Date: November 2026 • Version 1.0
Core Privacy Commitment: FileBrik is built from the ground up on a strict client-side architecture. We do not possess, operate, or maintain any backend servers that accept or store your uploaded files.

1. Technical Architecture & File Processing

When you use any utility on FileBrik (such as Compress PDF, Exam Resizer, or Audio Trimmer), your files are processed entirely inside your web browser's local sandbox memory using client-side WebAssembly and HTML5 Canvas APIs.

  • Zero Upload: Your files never transit across the internet to our systems or third-party servers.
  • Instant Deletion: As soon as you close or reload the browser tab, all temporary memory allocations are destroyed by your device's operating system.
  • Confidential Records: You can safely process bank statements, tax filings, legal agreements, and biometric photos with complete peace of mind.

2. Web Analytics & Aggregated Metrics

We respect user anonymity. We do not deploy invasive third-party fingerprinting trackers. We collect minimal aggregate telemetry (such as page views, country of origin, and tool usage frequencies) to monitor platform health and optimize tool performance.

3. Advertising Standards & Disclosures

FileBrik is sustained via non-intrusive programmatic advertisements. All advertising containers adhere to Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) safety standards and maintain reserved height boundaries to ensure a smooth reading experience. Legal, policy, and tool runner views maintain zero intrusive ad formats.

4. GDPR & CCPA Compliance

Because FileBrik does not ingest, transmit, or retain personal document files or personally identifiable information (PII) on backend infrastructure, we are GDPR and CCPA compliant by architectural design.
