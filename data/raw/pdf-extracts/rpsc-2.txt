Guidelines for candidates regarding OTR based online application and other services Page 5 of 47 2.1 Aadhaar based creation Candidate may select as ‘Aadhaar’ as Verification Type, enter Aadhaar number and click on “ Send OTP ” button. After Successful verification of entered OTP, all details which includes candidate’s name, DOB, Gender and Father’s name (If available), will be directly fetched from Aadhaar and now candidate needs to select his category, marital status and specially abled category. Email ID will be fetched from SSO profile. After Entering details, click on “Next” button. 2.2 Jan Aadhaar based creation (only for Rajasthan State) Candidate may select as ‘Jan - Aadhaar’ as Verification Type, enter Jan Aadhaar Number / Receipt Number and then by clicking on “ Fetch data ” button, a pop up will be opened to select member for which candidate need to create OTR profile. Figure 5 : JanAadhaar Member Selection Popup After selecting member, candidate need to click on “ Send OTP ” button. After entering OTP, all the details include candidate name, Date of birth, gender, father name, mobile number and Email (If updated on JanAadhaar) gets verified and will be directly fetched from JanAadhaar and then the candidate needs to select his category, marital status and specially abled category. Email will be fetched from SSO profile. After Entering details, click on “Next” button. 2.3 SSO profile based creation By selecting this option, candidate's name, date of birth, gender, mobile number and email ID (if available on SSO profile) will be directly fetched from SSO profile. After that the candidate must select only father's name, category, marital status and disabled category. The email will be fetched from the SSO profile. After entering the details, click on the "Next" button . Please note that if candidate has already created OTR with own Aadhaar / JanAadhaar on some other SSO ID and again tries to create new OTR profile from other SSO then screen with below mentioned details will be displayed after OTP verification.

My applications Candidates/kiosks can perform various tasks by clicking the " My Recruitment " link on the Recruitment Portal dashboard. The whole process of Application Editing is explained in 5 th and Application Withdrawal process is explained in 6 th chapter. 4.2 When Admit card is released by the Commission, then candidates can View/Download their Admit Card details by clicking on “ Get Admit Card ” tab. After Examination, the “ Get Roll Number ” button is shown, from which candidate can view their Roll Number in case they forget it. 4.3 If the candidate wants to submit Objections on the Question Paper and Answer Key release by the Commission after the examination, then he/she can click on “ Question Objection ” tab. Process To fill Offline Detailed Application Form: If the Candidate is directed to download and fill the Detailed-cum- Scrutiny form in Offline mode, then the candidate can download the offline form available on the RPSC Official Portal link ( https://rpsc.rajasthan.gov.in/downloads ) and then under the “General Information” section, click on the link name “ Instructions for Applicants (Online Application, Rules, Online Objection, Detailed Form, etc. )” and download the latest version of pdf and take the printout from page number 50 till end of the document and then fill this form. For payment purpose, the candidate can refer Chapter 8 “ Payment for Offline Services ”, pay ₹ 50 fee and attach the fee receipt with the Detailed Application form cum Scrutiny and submit all documents offline at RPSC office.
